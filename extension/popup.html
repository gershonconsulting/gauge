Before your first grade

When you click Grade me, Gauge opens your own LinkedIn pages in a background tab, one at a time, and reads these figures:

  • Your Social Selling Index and its four parts
  • How many invitations are pending, and how old they are
  • Your post impressions, engagements, followers and profile viewers
  • How many Sales Navigator lists you have and when they were updated
  • Your InMail credits and saved leads and accounts

It does not keep names of your leads or contacts, does not read your messages, and never clicks, sends or posts anything. Your report opens at gauge.gershon.ai and the grade is worked out in that tab.
