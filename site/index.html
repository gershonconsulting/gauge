How it works

3 steps to your score.

1

Sign in with LinkedIn

Gauge only receives your name, photo and email — so it can keep your scores and your history.

2

Install the extension

Add the Gauge extension to Chrome, open it and press Grade me. It reads your own pages, then closes them.

3

Get your grade

Your capacity score, your grade and your top 3 moves appear in your dashboard. Test again anytime to track progress.
