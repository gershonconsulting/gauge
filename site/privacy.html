Privacy policy

Draft, last updated November 14, 2026. To be reviewed before the Chrome Web Store submission.

Gauge (gauge.gershon.ai) is operated by Gershon Consulting LLC. It grades how efficiently you use LinkedIn Sales Navigator.

What the website receives

When you sign in with LinkedIn, LinkedIn sends Gauge your name, profile photo and email address. Gauge keeps these in a signed cookie in your browser for up to 30 days so you stay signed in. The LinkedIn access token is discarded immediately after sign-in. Gauge does not ask for permission to post, comment or react on your behalf.

What Gauge does not do

  • It does not post, send messages or send invitations.
  • It does not collect your leads, contacts or message content.
  • It does not sell or share your data with third parties.

Deleting your data

Signing out removes the cookie. For any request about your data, write to olivier@gershonconsulting.com.
