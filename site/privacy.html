Gauge by Gershon.ai

Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026. This policy covers the website gauge.gershon.ai and the Gauge Chrome extension.

Who we are

Gauge is operated by Gershon Consulting LLC, a Delaware (USA) company, which is the controller of the data described here. Contact for anything about privacy: olivier@gershonconsulting.com.

What Gauge is for

Gauge has one purpose: to show you a grade on how efficiently you use LinkedIn Sales Navigator, with tips to improve. Everything it collects is used for that purpose only.

What the website collects

When you choose "Sign in with LinkedIn", LinkedIn sends Gauge your name, profile photo address, email address and LinkedIn member identifier. Gauge asks LinkedIn for nothing else and never asks for permission to post, comment, react or message on your behalf. The LinkedIn access token is used once to read those four items and is then discarded; it is not stored.

Gauge keeps those items in one signed, secure cookie (gauge_session) in your browser for up to 30 days so you stay signed in. A second cookie (gauge_oauth_state) lasts up to 10 minutes during sign-in to protect it against forgery. Gauge uses no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies and no third-party trackers.

What the Chrome extension collects

The extension does nothing until you click "Grade me". When you click, it reads, from LinkedIn and Sales Navigator pages open in your own signed-in browser, figures about your own activity only:

  • your Social Selling Index and its four parts, and your industry and network rank;
  • the number and age of your pending invitations;
  • your content figures: impressions, engagements, number of posts and comments, followers, profile viewers and search appearances;
  • counts of your saved leads, saved accounts and saved searches, when your lists were last updated, and your remaining InMail credits.

These are counts, dates and scores. The extension sends them over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to gauge.gershon.ai, which calculates your grade and shows it to you.

The extension does not read or collect: the names or details of your leads, contacts or connections; the content of your messages or InMails; your password or LinkedIn session cookies; your browsing history; or anything on any website other than linkedin.com and gauge.gershon.ai. It does not send invitations or messages, does not post, does not click anything on your behalf, and does not run in the background.

What Gauge stores

For each graded account Gauge stores: your name, email address, LinkedIn member identifier, your overall score and grade, your five area scores, and the date of the grading. The individual figures listed above are used to calculate the grade and are not kept after that.

How Gauge uses your data

  • To sign you in and show you your grade and tips.
  • To keep the service secure and working (for example, investigating abuse or errors).
  • To email you about your results or about Gershon Consulting's services only if you ask for it, for example by booking a call. You can stop this at any time.

Gauge does not use your data for advertising, does not use it to assess credit-worthiness or lending, and does not build a profile of you for any other purpose.

Who your data is shared with

Gauge does not sell your data and does not transfer it to advertisers, data brokers or other information resellers. It is shared only with:

  • Cloudflare, Inc., which hosts the website and its storage on our behalf;
  • LinkedIn, which you sign in with (LinkedIn learns that you signed in to Gauge);
  • authorities, where the law requires it;
  • a successor company in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, and only after asking for your explicit consent.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received by the Gauge extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, nobody at Gershon Consulting reads your individual data unless you give explicit consent (for example, when you ask for help), it is needed for security purposes or to comply with the law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

How long data is kept

The sign-in cookie expires after 30 days or when you sign out. Stored grades are kept until you ask for deletion, and are deleted automatically after 24 months without a new grading.

Your choices and rights

You can sign out at any time, remove the extension at any time (which stops all collection), and ask us to show, correct, export or delete the data held about you by writing to olivier@gershonconsulting.com. We answer within 30 days. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, these are your rights under the GDPR; you may also object to or restrict processing, and complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in California, you have the rights to know, delete and correct under the CCPA; Gauge does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined there, and will not treat you differently for using your rights.

Legal bases under the GDPR: your consent (signing in and clicking "Grade me"; you can withdraw it at any time), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Where data is processed

Gershon Consulting is based in the United States and Cloudflare processes data in the United States and other countries. Where data leaves the EEA, UK or Switzerland, it is protected by Cloudflare's data processing terms, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. The sign-in cookie is signed, marked Secure and HttpOnly, and cannot be read by scripts. The extension contains all of its code; it loads no code from the internet.

Children

Gauge is a business tool and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes
