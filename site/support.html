Gauge by Gershon.ai

Support

Questions, a problem, or a request about your data: write to olivier@gershonconsulting.com. We answer within two business days.

Remove the extension

In Chrome, open the Extensions menu, find Gauge, and choose "Remove from Chrome". This stops all collection immediately.

Sign out

Use the "Sign out" button at the top of gauge.gershon.ai. This deletes the sign-in cookie.

Delete your data

Email olivier@gershonconsulting.com from the address you signed in with and ask for deletion. Your stored grade and details are deleted within 30 days. See the privacy policy.
